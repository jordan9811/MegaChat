# MegaChat brief — 2026-07-25

## Objectives today
- Stop MegaChat burning money on video infrastructure
- Prove the fix actually works
- Build the fan bounty mechanic (fans record clips for streamers who aren't on the platform yet)

## What happened
- Found the leak. The overlay page connected to the video service the moment OBS opened and never disconnected. A crash-reload loop then stacked ghost users on top. One session ran 30 hours with nobody in it.
- Fixed it. Overlay now stays disconnected until someone actually pays to join.
- Added guardrails: a timer that releases people who bail mid-payment, a spending circuit breaker, and an alarm on any long session. That alarm would have caught the 30-hour one on day one instead of month one.
- Built the bounty system end to end with fake money. Escrow, claims, payout, admin.
- Caught a design flaw in the spec: it paid streamers for having the logo on screen, not for playing the fans' clips. Park it for three hours, collect. Rewrote so the proof code only exists while a clip is playing.

## Problems and how we handled them
- Quota was half-rejecting connections for most of the day. The agent refused to run the verification rather than produce a green result that meant nothing. Right call. A prorated paid plan was purchased the same day, and it is unclear whether it was active during the measurement, so the test needs re-running either way.
- No way to pull LiveKit's usage numbers programmatically at this plan tier. Workaround: their webhooks push the data to us instead, which is why activating that URL matters.
- Ledger could corrupt on a crash and silently change everyone's balances. Rewritten so it refuses to start rather than serve wrong numbers.

## Outstanding
- Owner: paste the webhook URL into LiveKit. Two minutes. Protection is off until then.
- Diagnose whether the rejections clear now that a paid plan is active. The measurement predates the purchase.
- Four bounty fixes flagged, none urgent.
- Run B (real streamer sign-in, real video verification) needs platform apps registered and a test stream.
- Product call for the owner: what happens to money fans contributed for clips under 3 seconds, which are too short to verify.

## Outlook: mid, leaning bullish
- Bullish: the expensive bug is fixed and understood, guardrails exist, the bounty mechanic is built and its worst design flaw was caught before real money touched it.
- Mid: the core cost claim has still never been tested against LiveKit's own numbers, only against a local test server. It's believed to work, not shown to.
- Bearish only if: the 50% rejection turns out to be something structural rather than a leftover quota state. That would block real testing entirely.
