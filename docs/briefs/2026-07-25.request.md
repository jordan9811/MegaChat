# Request that triggered the 2026-07-25 brief

Kept verbatim, because the ask itself defines the format better than any style
guide would: the reader had been away all day, wanted no jargon, and wanted a
verdict rather than a status dump.

> i sent it how far is this gonna get us. also i havent been paying attention
> today just mbeen making yall figure iut out between you bc kinda boring so
> give me plain english summary in bullets summon i have add skill and breif me
> like im SVP and ur analyst on what happened today objectives, status,
> problems, how we solved them outstanding issues, outlook bbearishing mid
> bullish

Structure this implies, and the reason each section exists:
- **Objectives** — what the day was for, in one line each.
- **What happened** — outcomes, not activity. No commit logs.
- **Problems and how we handled them** — including the ones still unresolved.
- **Outstanding** — split by who has to act, owner vs engineering.
- **Outlook** — an actual call (bearish / mid / bullish) with the reasoning,
  including what would move it.

The reader is the project owner catching up, not an engineer reviewing work.
