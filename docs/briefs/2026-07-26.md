## What happened
- **The core claim is now proven.** Ran the overlay against the real LiveKit service for ten minutes with nobody in the room, checking from their side rather than ours: zero connections the entire time, twenty-nine checks in a row. Then a guest arrived, it connected, the guest left, it disconnected. This was the last thing in the project that was believed rather than shown.
- **Yesterday's worst case didn't happen.** The connections that were half-failing were simply the free plan running out. The paid plan fixed it — twelve rapid attempts and ten spaced attempts all succeeded. Not a deeper problem.
- Found and fixed a live bug while checking something else: if a streamer played the same fan clip twice, **the second play earned them nothing**. The system was looking up the first play's record and finding it already finished. Each play now gets its own identity.
- Hardened the proof records. The clips' verification codes are what a payout is calculated from, and they could previously be damaged by a crash without anyone noticing — which would quietly *underpay* a streamer. They now can't be altered without the system refusing to run, and a payout is blocked outright if the proof doesn't hold up.
- Wrote up the sub-3-second clip question for you to decide.
