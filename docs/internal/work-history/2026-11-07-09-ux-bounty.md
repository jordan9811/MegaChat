# 2026-11-07 → 11-09 — The UX bug bounty and the owner's picks

A UX sweep of every page (ten reviewers, each finding re-checked by a second)
produced 156 findings, published as a pick-one-per-card board. The owner picked
in three rounds; every pick below is built and live. Judgment calls are in
`DECISIONS.md` (2026-11-08, 2026-11-09); follow-ups in `OPEN-ISSUES.md` (HB1–HB6,
and the 2026-11-09 round-3 entry).

## Round 1 — 9 picks (`83665c8`)

- **Hollers bill by the second**, like Call in: rate × whole seconds recorded,
  capped at the longest clip (`letterPriceFor(cfg, seconds)`, `rooms-store.js`).
  A take airs for the seconds it was billed. Before, every take paid the longest
  clip's price.
- Room page: each button carries its own price; the shared "Room rate" box is
  gone; Send shows the take's price; the entry button hides while recording;
  Re-record asks first; Offline badge and one off-air line; paused rooms say so
  on both buttons.
- Dashboard: End room confirms in a modal; the Twitch channel sits in the follow
  switch's row.

## Round 2 — 23 picks (`776bde8`)

- Room page: one Sign in (asked at pay); no demo banner; no "no wallet" line;
  Twitch player only while live; actions first on phones and tablets; results
  under the control that caused them; Go Live on the camera frame; Enter in the
  name field starts nothing; one-line seat cost and a leave receipt; plain
  earned label; in-place "Add payout wallet".
- Create/dashboard: tap-to-toggle info tips that take no layout when closed;
  Create explains a short password; New room in the account menu and switcher;
  price fields stack on tablets; "Create room" / "Join" wording; Cash limits per
  field; Cash disabled and refused at save where it can never pay; reward terms
  on the board card; Holler credit retired; sound toggle beside the landing
  film's replay (the film file has no audio yet).
- Server: a points seat whose tick rounds to 0 points is refused (a never-seen
  wallet got a free seat in sub-dollar credit rooms).

## Round 3 — 100 picks

**Dashboard.** A failed room list shows "Couldn't load your rooms · Retry", never
a blank create form. Status reads "Open · waiting for your stream" or "On air".
The layout preview draws the bounty badge where it airs; one corner picker
offers only valid fill directions; the dead "Holler clips: own size and corner"
fields are gone. Pay every is a 30 s–10 min control. Rewards show their cost,
validate each field as you type, and keep each type's amount and cap. While
live, the review queue and on-camera table lift to the top when something
waits, and the status bar counts them. Queue rows read "name · 8 s · $0.15".
Room recovery accepts any link host and folds after unlocking. A bad payout
wallet errors at its field and no longer blocks other settings. Call in is
priced per 10 s like Hollers (the seat-time stepper is gone). "Pause now" during
the stream-ended countdown. Only the switch toggles a feature. Free keeps the
price to restore. One column at 900 px puts the live desk first; the layout
canvas is fluid; touch targets are 44 px. The OBS card sits at the top of the
right column.

**Overlay and OBS.** A Holler on screen counts as a taken tile, and a full room
refuses a Call in before any charge (`_gate-seat-tile-count.mjs`). A link with
no room or an unknown room draws nothing (no test-room fallback, no site 404 on
stream); "Re-copy overlay link" on the dashboard. Names 24 px at 1080p, up to
25 characters. Connecting seats and loading clips show designed cards, never an
empty or black frame. "Send a test Holler" plays a sample clip and test tiles,
unpaid. Tiles scale with the source width; a non-16:9 source is flagged on the
dashboard. "Badge too small" moves off air to the dashboard, clears as soon as
the code measures big enough, and no longer misfires on the text fallback badge.
Connect OBS is three short steps; the stuck-clip note links the OBS overlay and
asks before airing with no overlay.

**Room page.** Rewards have their own line with live states (earning, paused,
maxed, another tab) and "Sign in to start"; the seat meter shows only with a
seat. Refusals are sentences with a next step. The strip stays on one line.
Holler and Call in buttons use drawn marks. Free rooms say "free". The
entrance/exit picker moved into the Call in camera step and the Holler review
step. A bad room link shows "No room at this link".

**Board and site.** The On air toggle is gone (the header count stays). Tiles
show Holler / Call in / Rewards as three colour squares on the thumbnail with a
key in the filter row, and a host line under the name. "On air" is tally red
everywhere. Liveness badges read "Unverified" / "Hollers on" / "Verified" with
a tap or hover explanation. Featured card prices sit next to their names. The
404 points at /app with a real title and footer; /rooms and /bounties redirect.
The FAQ says bounties are real USDC. How it works has "For viewers" / "For
streamers" headings. The legacy-site footer link, the SIMPLE/ADV switch and the
roadmap legend line are gone. Sign-in lists Twitch first. Headers on bounty,
account and campaigns stop wrapping; the account chip shrinks on phones.

**Bounties.** The amount is a real money field with presets, and the server
refuses anything but a clean decimal before writing (`bounty-routes.js`,
`bounty-escrow.js`). One "Sign in to pay" button becomes Pay after sign-in. The
release-tier choice sits in the form. The recorder opens on the camera with
Record on the frame. After paying, "Send {handle} the link". New pools show
"Record the first Holler", no dead claim button. Placeholders, then Retry, when
a pool fails to load. One word, "Locked", in green. Rows sort by locked money.
Pasted channel links and search-as-you-type in Add a streamer. My bounties in
the account menu.

**Campaigns and account.** Places = budget ÷ reward (max claims is an optional
lower cap), so a campaign never promises more than it can pay. The form
defaults to your own room. Never-funded drafts can be deleted. The creator
previews a draft's offer page. "Pays out automatically" or "Payouts queued".
Signed-out visitors can build a campaign and sign in to save. The save bar
lists what's missing. Rules are grouped. A Rewards section on /account shows
what you earned per room.

## Not built, on purpose

- Custom token / stablecoin / seconds-credit rewards (rw-token-never-paid): a
  new money system, written up for the owner to approve.
- Drag-to-place, resizable overlay tiles (owner idea): tile size floors protect
  bounty verification.
- Flagged by the owner to circle back: the landing's three cards, the Docs link
  (GitBook), the example bounty rows (fund before launch), the room-type chips,
  the MEGACHAT badge mark, the overlay health pin.
