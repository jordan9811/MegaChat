# Limitations of verification

What the pipeline on the [previous page](README.md) cannot see (`bounty-confidence.js`, "WHAT EACH SIGNAL ACTUALLY PROVES"), with what is done about each and what remains. Each row carries an id from the internal *limitations register*, which holds the full entry: source, mitigation, residual risk, and what would close it.

| Id | Limitation | Mitigation in place | Residual risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | **No stranger has been verified.** Every real-broadcast proof was the operator's own channel or a rehearsal harness (`OPEN-ISSUES.md`, 2026-08-26 → 09-03). | The pipeline is exercised end to end by `_gate-run-b-pipeline.mjs` and by the rehearsal harnesses; every money path is on the retest list in `docs/internal/launch-readiness.md`. | A configuration, platform or network shape the operator never produced. |
| L2 | **Self-capture assumes a broadcast delay of at least 12 s.** The freeze delay is derived for D ∈ [12, 45] s; at D = 0 a 30-second clip loses its first 6 s (`bounty-claim.config.js`, "WHAT 51s DOES NOT COVER"). | 12 s is the floor measured on a real broadcast; every stub in the gates publishes at ~0 s and passes only because its clips are short. | A platform faster than any measured, or a future clip cap above 30 s, breaks the derivation silently unless both values are re-derived together. |
| L3 | **Kick self-capture underperformed the Twitch archive path** on the first real run — 1 of 5 against 4 of 5 (`OPEN-ISSUES.md`, "KICK'S FIRST REAL BROADCAST"). | The freeze window was re-derived from the measured delay spread in Pass A (`b3ccf8b`); Kick samples at 2× density. | Not re-measured on Kick since the re-derivation. |
| L4 | **The OBS scene check is client-reported.** A determined cheat can post whatever they like (`web/lib/obs-scene-check.mjs`). | It is never a hard gate and never the only thing holding a verification up; it raises confidence and diagnoses accidents (`bounty-confidence.js`). | None for payout — it cannot cause one on its own. Support quality depends on it. |
| L5 | **Self-capture is MegaChat's own artefact.** An auditor must trust that MegaChat recorded what it says it did (`bounty-confidence.js`, tier 3). | Captures are content on disk with freshness checks (`_gate-stale-capture.mjs`); where a platform keeps a replay, the external read is preferred and the two are cross-checked (`OPEN-ISSUES.md`, 2026-08-30). | On X and Kick there is no external copy to cross-check against. |
| L6 | **A badge can be legible while the clip is covered.** The reader finds the badge; it does not judge whether the guest's video was visible. | The badge is drawn inside the tile, so a covered tile usually covers the badge too. Not measured. | A layout that reveals the badge and hides the video verifies. |
| L7 | **720p minimum.** At 480p glyphs land at ~12 px, on the reading floor, and detection drops (`docs/run-b-verification.md`). | The streamer is warned about stream quality before going live (`_gate-quality-and-platform.mjs`, "the two things a streamer must be TOLD"). | 480p-only streamers verify marginally or not at all. |
| L8 | **The synthetic corpus once hid a decoder bug** — it used one code, and a ~50% miss rate at 720p went unseen (`5ebd643`; `OPEN-ISSUES.md`, "the corpus measured one code"). | `_gate-decoder-codes.mjs` sweeps detection across codes. | Any other property the corpus holds fixed is a property nobody has varied. |
| L9 | **Stubs publish instantly.** Every HLS stub in the gates has D ≈ 0, so three delay-dependent bugs passed green for a month (`OPEN-ISSUES.md`, the real-broadcast entries; `_gate-broadcast-delay.mjs` exists because of it). | `_gate-broadcast-delay.mjs` runs self-capture against a lagging stub. | A gate that does not use the lagging stub proves nothing about delay. |
| L10 | **Settlement is a stub.** Nothing on this page results in money moving (`bounty-settlement.js`). | By design; Gate H asserts it on every run. | The real settlement path, when written, is untested by definition. |
| L11 | **The evidence chain is local.** It is append-only and checksummed, and it lives on one volume (`bounty-evidence.js`; `bounty-ledger.js`). | Torn-tail recovery and interior-corruption refusal are gated (`_gate-bounty-claim.mjs`, section J). | Loss of the volume is loss of the evidence. Nothing replicates it. |
| L12 | **Retention is 14 days.** Captures are swept at `captureRetentionMs` and purged with their pledge (`bounty-capture.js`). | `claimTtlMs` is also 14 days, so a capture does not expire before the claim it supports. | A dispute raised after the window has no frames to re-read. |
| L13 | **The badge does not move with the layout.** It is fixed at bottom-left (`public/overlay.html`, `#bounty-badge`); a room whose tile stack starts bottom-left covers it. | None in code. The default origin is top-right, so an unedited room is unaffected. | A bounty room with a bottom-left origin fails verification for an honest streamer. Recorded in `OPEN-ISSUES.md` (2026-09-16). |

Two further limitations are about the *program*, not the pipeline, and are on the [Bounty program](../features/bounty-program.md) page: the obs-websocket path is untested against a real OBS during a real broadcast, and Rumble cannot be verified today.
